# Content Marketing Strategy

## Overview
Build a complete content marketing strategy: audience research, content vision, keyword targeting, editorial planning, content production workflows, distribution amplification, and measurement frameworks aligned to business growth.

## When to Use
- "Create a content marketing strategy for my business"
- "How do I plan content that actually grows my business?"
- "Build a content calendar that works"
- "Content SEO strategy"

## Phase 1: Foundation

### Step 1: Business & Audience Alignment
Connect content to business goals, then to audience needs.

**Business Goals → Content Objectives:**
- Revenue → Lead generation (top-of-funnel) + conversion (bottom-of-funnel)
- Brand awareness → Educational content, thought leadership
- Market education → Explainer content, problem/solution content
- Customer retention → Onboarding, advanced tutorials, community content

**Audience Research Framework:**
1. **Demographics**: Age, location, job title, industry, income level
2. **Psychographics**: Goals, challenges, values, content consumption habits
3. **Content preferences**: Formats they prefer (video, long-form, short posts)
4. **Pain points**: Specific problems preventing goals
5. **Information sources**: Where they get info (search, social, podcasts)
6. **Decision process**: How they buy (self-research, consult reviews, demo)

### Step 2: Brand Positioning & Voice
Document these three elements:

**Brand Positioning Statement:**
"For [target audience] who want to [desired outcome], [your brand] is the [category] that [unique differentiator] unlike [alternatives] which [reason to believe]."

**Content Vision:**
"What we want our brand to be known for in the minds of our audience."
Example: "The go-to resource for [topic] that helps [audience] achieve [outcome] without [common obstacle]."

**Content Personality Matrix:**
| Dimension | Low End | High End | Our Brand |

| Formal/Casual | Academic, formal | Conversational, casual | |
| Serious/Playful | Serious, professional | Fun, playful | |
| Authoritative/Helpful | Expert, directive | Friendly, supportive | |
| Abstract/Concrete | Conceptual, theoretical | Practical, actionable | |

## Phase 2: Content Planning

### Topic Research & Keyword Mapping

#### SEO Keyword Framework (use with seo-strategy skill)
| Keyword Type | Intent | Search Volume | Difficulty |
|--------------|--------|---------------|------------|
| Pillar keywords | High-level topic | 10K-100K | 50-80 |
| Cluster keywords | Subtopics | 1K-10K | 30-60 |
| Long-tail keywords | Specific queries | 100-1K | 10-40 |
| Branded keywords | Your brand terms | Variable | 0-20 |

#### Keyword Research Process
1. **Seed keywords**: 20-30 core topics related to your business
   ```
   Example: SEO → "keyword research," "on-page SEO," "SEO audit," "SEO tools"
   ```

2. **Expand with long-tail**: Use `blog-idea-generator` and keyword tools
   ```
   "how to do keyword research for SEO" 
   "SEO audit checklist for beginners"
   ```

3. **Map to funnel stages**:
   - **Awareness** (top of funnel): Questions, "how to," "what is"
   - **Consideration** (middle): Comparisons, reviews, "best," "vs"
   - **Decision** (bottom): Pricing, demos, "buy," "sign up"

### Content Pillar Strategy
Define 3-5 pillars (content categories) covering your topic:

**Pillar Example: SaaS Marketing**
1. **Growth Channels**: SEO, content marketing, paid ads, partnerships
2. **Conversion Optimization**: Landing pages, email funnels, copywriting
3. **Retention & Expansion**: Onboarding, churn reduction, upselling
4. **Tools & Technology**: Stack reviews, integration guides, case studies
5. **Team & Process**: Hiring, management, workflows

**Pillar Page Structure:**
```
Pillar: [Topic]
├── Ultimate Guide to [Topic] (long-form pillar page)
│   ├── Subtopic 1 → Cluster content (blog post or video)
│   ├── Subtopic 2 → Cluster content
│   ├── Subtopic 3 → Cluster content
│   └── ...
└── Weekly content on subtopics
```

## Phase 3: Content Production

### Content Production Workflow
Use the `content-marketing-workflow` skill for detailed editorial workflows.

**Simplified 5-Stage Workflow:**
1. **Brainstorm**: Use `blog-idea-generator` → select 3 topics/week
2. **Write**: Draft with clear outline → get 2nd draft within 2 hours
3. **Review**: Peer review or self-edit → check for accuracy and voice
4. **Optimize**: SEO review → keyword placement, meta, internal links
5. **Publish**: Schedule across channels with social promotion plan

### Content Brief Template
```markdown
# Content Brief: [Title]

## Target Keyword: [Primary keyword]
## Search Intent: [Informational/Transactional/Navigational]
## Target Audience: [Persona + stage in buyer journey]

## Goal: [Specific outcome — drive traffic/generate leads/build authority]

## Key Points to Cover:
1. [Main point supported by data]
2. [Common misconception to correct]
3. [Actionable takeaway/step]
4. [Real example or case study]

## Target Word Count: [X words]

## Internal Links to Include:
- [Related existing content]
- [Pillar page on this topic]

## External Links to Include:
- [Authoritative source for data/claims]

## Call-to-Action:
[What readers should do after consuming]
```

### Repurposing Pipeline
Each piece of core content → 5-7 repurposed assets:
1. Blog post (core content)
2. LinkedIn post summary (300-500 words)
3. Twitter thread (8-12 tweets from key takeaways)
4. Email newsletter (curated insights from content)
5. Video (talking head explaining key point)
6. Infographic (data/visual summary)
7. Podcast episode (audio version)

## Phase 4: Distribution Amplification

### Owned Media Distribution
| Channel | Strategy | Frequency |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Blog** | Publish 2-3x/week, SEO-focused | 2-3 posts/week |
| **Email newsletter** | Curated content + original insights | Weekly |
| **YouTube** | Tutorial videos, repurposed blog content | 1-2 videos/week |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional insights, company updates | 3-5 posts/week |
| **Company blog** | Deep dives, original research | 1 post/week |

### Earned Media Strategy
- **Guest posting**: 1 post/month on relevant sites
- **Podcast appearances**: 1-2 podcasts/month as guest
- **Industry expert sources**: Respond to reporter queries (HARO, Qwoted)
- **Speaking opportunities**: Webinars, conferences, meetups
- **Awards/submissions**: Enter relevant industry awards

## Phase 5: Measurement & Optimization

### Content Marketing Metrics

#### Awareness Stage
| Metric | Target | Tool |
|--------|--------|------|
| Organic traffic | +25% YoY | Google Analytics |
| Blog views | 500-2000/view | GA4 |
| Time on page | >3 minutes | GA4 |

#### Consideration Stage
| Metric | Target | Tool |
|--------|--------|------|
| Email signups | 2-5% CTR | Email platform |
| Lead magnet conversions | >2% | Analytics |
| Return visitors | >30% | GA4 |

#### Decision Stage
| Metric | Target | Tool |
|--------|--------|------|
| Marketing-to-sales conversion | 10-20% | CRM |
| Demo/consultation requests | >5% from content | CRM |
| Content-influenced deals | Track % | CRM |

#### Retention/Advocacy Stage
| Metric | Target | Tool |
|--------|--------|------|
| Customer referrals | 20-30% | Referral program |
| Net Promoter Score | >50 | Survey tool |
| Repeat engagement | 40%+ | Analytics |

### Content Performance Analysis Framework
For each piece of content, analyze:

| Dimension | Metric | Good | Great |
|-----------|--------|------|-------|
| **Reach** | Page views, unique visitors | 500 views | 5000+ views |
| **Engagement** | Time on page, scroll depth | >3 min | >5 min + 70% scroll |
| **Social** | Shares, saves, comments | 2% share rate | 5%+ share rate |
| **SEO** | Keyword ranking, organic clicks | Top 10 | Top 3 |
| **Conversion** | CTR on CTAs, form submissions | 2% | 5%+ |
| **Authority** | Backlinks, branded mentions | 5 backlinks | 20+ backlinks |

### Optimization Cycle
1. **Month 1**: Launch with best practices
2. **Month 2-3**: Identify top 5 and bottom 5 performing pieces
3. **Month 4-5**: A/B test headlines, CTAs, and distribution
4. **Month 6**: Double down on top performers, pause bottom 5
5. **Quarterly**: Full content audit, strategy adjustment

## Common Pitfalls
1. **No connection to business goals** — content without a purpose is just noise
2. **Ignoring audience research** — assuming you know what they want vs asking
3. **Keyword stuffing instead of answering questions** — Google rewards helpful content
4. **No repurposing strategy** — each piece should yield 5-7 assets
5. **Inconsistent publishing** — weekly cadence matters more than volume
6. **Not tracking ROI** — measuring likes instead of pipeline contribution
7. **Topic drift** — content strays from core pillars and audience needs
8. **No distribution plan** — publishing without amplification is wasted effort
9. **Only promotional content** — audience needs 80% helpful, 20% promotional
10. **Skipping competitor analysis** — missing opportunities to differentiate
