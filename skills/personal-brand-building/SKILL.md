# Personal Brand Building

## Overview
Systematic personal brand development: niche definition (expertise × passion × market demand), platform strategy, content batching system, consistency cadence, networking strategy, authority building (speaking, writing, teaching), and brand asset creation (headshot, bio, banner, link-in-bio).

## When to Use
- Defining your personal brand niche and positioning
- Choosing which platforms to focus on
- Setting up a content batching and publishing system
- Building a daily networking habit
- Establishing authority through speaking, writing, teaching
- Creating consistent brand assets (headshot, bio, banners, link-in-bio)

## Body

### 1. Niche Definition

Find your intersection: **Expertise × Passion × Market Demand**

1. **Expertise**: What can you teach others? (skills, experience, knowledge)
2. **Passion**: What do you enjoy talking about? (you'll need this for consistency)
3. **Market demand**: What do people want to learn? (research trends, content gaps)

**Example:** "I help startup founders build product-led growth engines" = PLG expertise × startup passion × high demand from founders.

**Niche sharpening:**
```
Too broad:  "Marketing expert"
Better:    "B2B SaaS content marketing for seed-stage startups"
Best:      "Content-led sales for B2B SaaS startups with <$5M ARR"
```

### 2. Platform Strategy

| Platform | Best For | Content Format | Frequency |
|----------|----------|----------------|-----------|
| **LinkedIn** | B2B, professional, long-form | Posts, articles, carousels | 3–5x/week |
| **Twitter/X** | Real-time, networking, short-form | Threads, hot takes | 3–10x/day |
| **YouTube** | Depth, teaching, visibility | Tutorials, vlogs, deep dives | 1x/week |
| **Newsletter** | Direct audience, monetization | Essays, curated content | 1–2x/week |
| **Podcast** | Authority, network expansion | Interviews, solo episodes | 1x/week |

**Rule:** Master one platform as your "home base" first (6 months daily), then expand. Never start 3+ platforms at once.

### 3. Content Batching System

**Monthly batch day schedule:**
- **Brainstorm** (2h): Generate 30 ideas using frameworks: observations, questions, hot takes, teaching points, case studies, contrarian opinions
- **Write** (4h): Produce 1 week of content (5 posts or 20 tweets) in one sitting
- **Record** (3h): Film 4 videos or 2 podcast episodes
- **Design** (2h): Create visuals, carousels, thumbnails
- **Schedule** (1h): Load into scheduling tool (Buffer, Hootsuite, Later)

**Consistency cadence:**
- Minimum: 1x/week (builds habit)
- Growth: 3–5x/week (builds audience)
- Power: Daily (builds momentum)
- **Key insight**: Consistency > quality in the first 6 months

### 4. Networking Strategy

**Daily:** Comment on 5–10 posts from peers in your niche. Add value — don't just say "Great post!" Add a thought, question, or counterpoint.

**Weekly:** Share the spotlight — tag someone in every third post, amplify others' wins.

**Monthly:** Collaborate — guest on 1 podcast, co-write 1 article, do 1 Twitter Spaces or LinkedIn Live.

**Quarterly:** Attend 1–2 conferences or meetups in your niche. Follow up with new connections within 48h.

### 5. Authority Building

**Speaking:**
- Apply to speak at industry conferences (CFP process)
- Start with local meetups, virtual events, podcast guest appearances
- Prepare 3 talk titles with descriptions for your speaker page
- Record every talk for YouTube/portfolio

**Writing:**
- Guest post on industry blogs (reach larger audience + backlinks)
- Publish thought leadership articles on LinkedIn and Medium
- Contribute to industry publications (search "write for us" + your niche)

**Teaching:**
- Create a mini-course (free or paid) on your expertise
- Host workshops or webinars
- Mentor others (gives back + builds reputation)

### 6. Brand Assets

- **Headshot**: Professional, approachable, consistent across all platforms
- **Bio template**: "I help [audience] achieve [result] through [method]. [Personal detail.]"
- **Banner images**: Platform-specific dimensions (LinkedIn: 1584×396px, Twitter/X: 1500×500px)
- **Color palette**: 3 colors that reflect your brand personality
- **Link-in-bio page**: Carrd, Linktree, or bio.link with your offerings, social links, newsletter signup

## Common Pitfalls

- **Overthinking the start**: Your early content won't be perfect. Publish anyway.
- **Trying to be everywhere**: One great platform beats three mediocre ones.
- **No clear niche**: "Marketing expert" is too broad. Specificity builds authority.
- **Content without connection**: Posting without engaging in comments/DMs = broadcasting, not brand-building.
- **Inconsistency**: 3 months of daily posts then 6 months silence destroys algorithmic momentum.
- **Impatience**: Personal brands take 6–12 months of consistent effort to gain traction.
