# Freelance Business Operations

## Overview
A comprehensive operational framework for running a freelance or consulting business. Covers service packaging, pricing strategy, client onboarding, project management, invoicing, scope creep prevention, portfolio building, and testimonial collection.

## When to Use
- Setting up a new freelance business or re-evaluating operations
- Defining service packages and pricing (hourly, project, value-based)
- Onboarding a new client — contract, deposit, kickoff
- Managing projects and preventing scope creep
- Sending invoices and collecting payments
- Building a portfolio and gathering testimonials
- Auditing your freelance operations for efficiency

## Body

### 1. Service Packaging (Tiered Offerings)

Structure services into 3 tiers to anchor value:

| Tier | Name | Description | Price Multiplier |
|------|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Basic | Essentials | Core deliverable, fixed scope | 1x |
| Standard | Recommended | Essentials + extras, faster turnaround | 1.6x–2x |
| Premium | Full-Service | Everything + priority support, revisions | 2.5x–4x |

### 2. Pricing Strategy

**Three models:**

| Model | When | How | Pros | Cons |
|-------|------|-----|------|------|
| Hourly | Unclear scope, maintenance, ongoing | Track time (Toggl/Clockify), bill monthly | Simple, covers overruns | Penalizes efficiency, caps earnings |
| Project | Clear scope, fixed deliverable | Estimate hours × rate + 20% buffer | Predictable for client | Risk if scope grows |
| Value-Based | High-impact, measurable outcomes | Price = % of client's ROI from your work | Highest earnings | Harder to sell |

**Rate formula:** Target Income / Billable Days = Daily Rate → Daily Rate / Hours per Day = Hourly Rate. Example: $100k / 200 days / 6h = $83/h → round to $100/h. Raise rates 10–25% every 6–12 months or when at 80%+ capacity.

### 3. Client Onboarding

**Step 1 — Discovery call:** Understand pain, scope, timeline, budget. Key questions: "What's the #1 problem you're solving? What outcome makes this a success?" Do NOT quote on the call.

**Step 2 — Proposal:** Problem Statement → Solution → Timeline → Investment → Next Steps.

**Step 3 — Contract (always):** Scope of work, payment terms (30–50% deposit), revision caps, termination clause, IP transfer on final payment, NDA if needed.

**Step 4 — Deposit & kickoff:** Collect 30–50% upfront. Send invoice within 24h. Kickoff email with access requests and first milestone date.

### 4. Scope Creep Prevention

**Signals:** "While you're at it…", "small tweak", expanding requirements, email ping-pong.

**Tactics:** Contract lists what's NOT included. Change request process with cost + timeline impact. Revision caps in packages. Weekly recap reinforces scope.

**Change request template:**
```
Subject: Scope Change — [Project]
Description:
Timeline impact (+X days):
Added cost ($Y):
[ ] Proceed  [ ] Discuss
```

### 5. Invoicing

Net-15 terms. Late fee 1.5–5%/month. Pause work after 14 days overdue. Payment methods: Stripe, PayPal, bank transfer, Wise. Invoice on milestone completion immediately.

### 6. Portfolio & Testimonials

**Case study format:** Problem → Solution → Result. Quantify outcomes. 5–8 showcase projects. Ask for testimonial within 48h of positive delivery.

**Testimonial request:**
```
Subject: Quick feedback?

Hi [Client],

I'm building my portfolio — would you mind sharing 2-3 sentences about:
1. What problem I helped you solve
2. What it was like working with me
3. Any results you've seen

Even a 30s voice memo works — I'll transcribe it. Thanks!
```

## Common Pitfalls

- **No contract**: Verbal agreements fail. Always write it down.
- **Underpricing**: Freelancers undervalue by 30–50%. Use the rate formula.
- **No deposit**: Never start without 30–50% upfront.
- **Scope creep without billing**: Small changes add up. Bill everything outside scope.
- **No savings buffer**: Maintain 3–6 months of expenses. Income is lumpy.
- **Not raising rates**: Inflation + experience means rates must rise yearly.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] 3 service tiers with clear pricing
- [ ] Contract template ready (scope, deposit, revision caps, termination)
- [ ] Onboarding sequence documented (discovery → proposal → contract → deposit → kickoff)
- [ ] Invoicing tool set up with late payment terms
- [ ] Change request template ready
- [ ] Portfolio with 5+ case studies
- [ ] Testimonial collection process documented
- [ ] Rate review scheduled (6-month recurring)
